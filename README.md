# Sales and Finance Analytics

This project is an advanced-level data analytics initiative focused on Sales and Finance analysis for AtliQ Hardware, a company dealing in hardware and peripheral products. The main aim was to explore sales data in depth and generate meaningful reports that support better financial decision-making and help track important business KPIs.

### Problem Overview

AtliQ Hardware works with major clients like Croma, Best Buy, Staples, and Flipkart. However, their sales data is spread across multiple sources and lacks proper structure.

In this project, I worked with more than 500,000 sales records and used ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) techniques to clean and organize the data. The final reports provide clear insights into sales performance, customer behavior, and overall financial health. These insights help management make informed decisions and keep track of financial goals more effectively.

### Objectives

The core objective of this project was to analyze sales and financial data and build insightful reports that support data-driven decision-making.

Specifically, the project focused on:

Cleaning and organizing raw sales data using ETL processes
Creating performance reports for sales, customers, and markets
Identifying trends in sales growth, product performance, and profitability
Supporting improvements in net sales, gross margin, and overall financial performance
Data Preparation

The data preparation process involved three key ETL steps:

Extract: Collected raw data from multiple sources such as CSV and Excel files
Transform: Cleaned the data by removing duplicates, handling missing values, and structuring it properly
Load: Loaded the processed data into Excel for further analysis

I used Microsoft Excel along with Power Pivot for data modeling, and DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) to create calculated columns and measures. This helped in building dynamic and interactive reports that can support business decisions.

### Report Generation
Customer Performance Report

This report highlights Net Sales for different customers across the years 2019, 2020, and 2021. It also shows the percentage growth in performance from 2020 to 2021.


### Market Performance vs Targets Report 

This report compares Sales Targets vs Actual Performance for different countries in 2021. I calculated the difference between the actual sales and targets, along with the % of target achievement. 

### Top 10 Products Report

This report is to  identified the top 10 products based on the percentage increase in net sales from 2020 to 2021. This helps the company to understand which products are performing well and which ones are driving revenue growth.

### Fiscal Year and Monthly P/L Reports

This report provides the  insights for the Gross Margin for different markets, customers, divisions, and regions across fiscal years and months which  allows the company to understand its profitability trends.
